Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy applies to the Gamerz Android application and its supporting services and user-generated content.

Operator and contact

Gamerz is an independent gaming social app operated by an independent developer in Bahrain. Use the privacy inquiry form at the bottom of this page for data requests and our dedicated account deletion page to request account deletion. Never send passwords or verification codes. The developer identity and support details displayed in Google Play must match the actual operator.

Data we collect and why

We process email and sign-in information through Firebase Authentication to operate accounts; usernames, chosen gaming platforms, optional avatars, favorite games and series for profiles; game reviews, uploaded images, ratings, self-reported completion and platinum achievements, posts, comments, reactions, follows and stories for community features; direct messages, their participants, timestamps and read markers for messaging; private bookmarks, libraries and blocks for personal account tools; check-ins, tasks and cosmetic ranks for progression; reports, feedback and moderation actions for safety; and technical connection, device and app-integrity signals that Firebase and App Check may process to secure and troubleshoot the service. We do not directly collect payment card numbers or sell personal data.

Who can see what

Signed-in community members may see public usernames, profiles, chosen platforms, optional public images, reviews, stories, public activity and achievements. Sign-in email and credentials are not displayed in the public profile. Private libraries, saved posts, block lists, consent records and private activity timestamps are restricted to their owners by client access rules. Blocking does not remove copies or links previously shared outside Gamerz.

Messages, reports and moderation

Messages are stored in Firestore and client access is restricted to the conversation participants. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted; do not send sensitive secrets. When a message is reported, a verified copy of the reported text is stored in an owner-restricted report for moderation. Users may report posts, stories and messages and block accounts. The operator may remove content or suspend accounts after moderation review.

Service providers and international processing

Gamerz uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore and App Check) for accounts, app data and protection, and Supabase Storage (project region: India) for newly uploaded public post, story and profile images. Uploads and removals pass through an Edge Function that checks Firebase sign-in; privileged Supabase keys are not bundled in the app. The image bucket is public for viewing, so anyone who obtains an image link may view the image; do not upload sensitive or private photographs. Older Uploadcare media may remain available until separately migrated or deleted. Supabase does not delete those older files. Public policy/support pages may be hosted separately. If Google Play subscriptions are enabled in the future, Google Play will handle checkout and a trusted backend must verify entitlements. Providers may process data outside Bahrain under their terms and configurations.

Retention and images

Content and account activity are kept while needed to provide the service and until removed through available processes, subject to records necessary for abuse investigations or legal obligations. Stories disappear from the app interface after 24 hours, but their media files and caches may persist. Anyone with a public CDN image URL may still be able to open it. Service providers may retain temporary copies or logs according to their own retention rules. Full automatic cleanup of all older files, interactions and conversation records is not yet complete; deleting an account should not be mistaken for instantaneous removal of every copy.

Account and data deletion

Request account deletion inside the app in account settings or from our external account deletion page without installing the app. We verify account ownership before processing an external request. Available flows delete Firebase Authentication and some related data, but complete cleanup of older Uploadcare files, linked interactions, messages, moderation records and backups requires additional review and may need manual action. Submitting a request does not mean deletion is already complete. Where lawful retention is required, its scope should be explained during processing. Do not send passwords.

Your rights and security

You may request access, correction, deletion or applicable objections under relevant law. We verify identity before disclosing or modifying account data. We use authentication, access controls and HTTPS for data in transit, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not publish private information about yourself or others without permission.

Minors and policy updates

Gamerz does not currently offer an independently verified parental consent system and should not be treated as approved for targeting children until the applicable Google Play and legal requirements are met. Minors must involve a parent or guardian where required by applicable law. We update this policy when services, providers or data practices change and request renewed consent where necessary.

Privacy rights and contact

You can start account deletion from Gamerz account settings and read the deletion information below. A verified support email for requests outside the app must be added before public launch. Never disclose passwords or verification codes.
