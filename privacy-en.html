Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy applies to the Gamerz Android application and its supporting services and user-generated content.

Operator and contact

Gamerz is an Android social app for gamers operated by an independent publisher in Bahrain. For privacy inquiries or requests to access, correct or erase account data, contact official.gamerz.app@gmail.com or see the account deletion page. We may verify account ownership before handling an account-specific request. Never send passwords or verification codes.

Data we process and why

We use your registration email and Firebase Authentication sign-in data to maintain and secure your account. The username, gaming platforms, optional profile photo, favorite game and favorite series you choose are used to display and personalize your profile.

We process posts, selected photographs, stories, ratings, self-reported completion and platinum achievements, comments, likes, follows, saved posts, private libraries, tasks and ranks to provide community features. Message data includes message text, participant identifiers, timestamps and read markers. Reports, related evidence and moderation decisions help us handle abuse.

Firebase and App Check may process technical app, device, network-address and integrity-token data to operate, secure and diagnose the service. Current Android permissions do not request your contacts or precise location. We do not directly collect payment-card numbers or sell personal information.

Who can see what

Signed-in members can see public usernames, profiles, chosen platforms, public favorites, images, posts, stories, visible interactions and achievements. Sign-in email and passwords are not shown in the public profile. Access to private libraries, bookmarks, blocked-user lists and consent records is restricted to the account owner, while moderation reports are restricted to authorized reviewers. Public media URLs may be accessible to anyone who possesses the link, including people outside Gamerz. Blocking someone does not retract copies or links shared earlier.

Messages, reports and moderation

Messages are stored in Firestore and the app's database access rules restrict reading to the conversation participants. Messaging is not end-to-end encrypted (E2EE); do not send passwords or highly sensitive secrets. When a message is reported, a copy of the reported text, the relevant identifiers and the reason may be available to an authorized moderator; this does not grant a general right to browse every private conversation. Reports, blocking and moderation are used to address misuse, including removal of content or limited account restrictions. Activity and message alerts appear inside the app; delivery as external push notifications is not guaranteed.

Service providers and international processing

We use Google Firebase, including Authentication, Cloud Firestore and App Check, for accounts, community information and access controls. Images submitted through the current upload service are stored in Supabase Storage (project region: India) using a server endpoint that verifies the account. Older images may remain hosted by Uploadcare or Cloudinary until separately removed from those services; changing providers does not erase historical media links. This policy and related legal pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Where in-app subscription purchases are offered, Google Play processes payment and Gamerz processes membership entitlement status without receiving your card number. Providers may process data outside Bahrain under their policies and configurations.

Data and image retention

We keep account information, content and interactions while required to provide the service and until removal through applicable procedures or a valid request, subject to specific legal or security reasons to retain limited records. Stories leave the in-app display after 24 hours, but related files, cached copies, records and provider backups may persist longer. Removing a post or story from a screen does not prove all copies were erased. Abuse reports and security records are retained only as needed for legitimate purposes. You can ask support about deletion progress or remaining data; we cannot promise immediate removal of third-party caches and backups.

Account and data deletion

Start account deletion in Gamerz settings or request it outside the app through the deletion page or official.gamerz.app@gmail.com. We verify account ownership. Requests cover the sign-in account, profile, posts, stories, associated interactions, messages and media under the applicable processes; deleting a shared conversation may also affect another participant's view. Historical-provider media and cached copies may require separate follow-up. A submitted request or disappearance from the app is not itself proof that every step succeeded. Ask for confirmation and the status of any remaining data. Limited information may be retained for a specific legitimate security or legal reason.

Your rights and security

You can edit certain profile details in the app and request access, correction, deletion or objection where applicable law provides those rights. We may verify account ownership before releasing or changing account information. Firebase authentication, access restrictions, HTTPS transport and provider safeguards help protect data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Report privacy concerns to the support contact above.

Age suitability and protection of minors

Gamerz is a gaming community and does not automatically restrict access to adults only. Minors and their guardians must comply with applicable local age requirements, store classification and legally required consent. Gamerz does not currently independently verify parental consent; having a minor account does not itself establish that valid consent has been obtained. Abusive and exploitative content is prohibited and reporting and blocking tools are available. Material changes in data practices will be published here, with renewed consent where required.

Privacy rights and contact

For privacy enquiries, data requests, and deletion: official.gamerz.app@gmail.com. See account and data deletion instructions. The email link opens your mail app; this webpage does not collect passwords, verification codes or account data.
