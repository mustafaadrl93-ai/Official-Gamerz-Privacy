Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy applies to the Gamerz Android application and its supporting services and user-generated content.

Operator and contact

Gamerz is an independent gaming social app operated by an independent developer in Bahrain. The support and privacy request contact is official.gamerz.app@gmail.com. You may email us to request access, correction or deletion of your data. See account deletion instructions. Never share your password or verification codes. The developer's legal identity disclosed on Google Play must match the actual operator.

Data we process and why

We use your email address and Firebase Authentication sign-in information to operate accounts; your username, gaming platform, and optional avatar, favorite game and series to personalize profiles. We process game posts, user-selected uploaded images, ratings, self-reported completion and platinum achievements, comments, likes, follows and stories to run community features; messages, participants, timestamps and read indicators for messaging; bookmarks, libraries, blocks, tasks and ranks for account features; and reports, associated evidence and moderation actions to address abuse. Firebase and App Check may process technical information such as connection addresses, device signals and app-integrity tokens for security and diagnostics. The app's current Android permissions do not request access to contacts or location. We do not directly collect payment card details or sell personal data.

Who can see what

Signed-in community members may see public usernames, profiles, chosen platforms, optional public images, reviews, stories, public activity and achievements. Sign-in email and credentials are not displayed in the public profile. Private libraries, saved posts, block lists, consent records and private activity timestamps are restricted to their owners by client access rules. Blocking does not remove copies or links previously shared outside Gamerz.

Messages, reports and moderation

Messages are stored in Firestore and client access is restricted to the conversation participants. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted; do not send sensitive secrets. When a message is reported, a verified copy of the reported text is stored in an owner-restricted report for moderation. Users may report posts, stories and messages and block accounts. The operator may remove content or suspend accounts after moderation review.

Service providers and international processing

We use Google Firebase, including Authentication for sign-in, Firestore for account and community data, and App Check to verify app requests. We use Supabase Storage and its server functions to store, upload and delete newly uploaded images after checking the user's identity; the current storage project is hosted in India. Public post, story and profile images may be viewed by anyone who has their URL; do not upload private or sensitive photographs. Some older images may remain with a previous storage provider until separately removed at their source. Moving new uploads to Supabase does not automatically delete historical media. We host policy pages through GitHub Pages. Payments for any subscription available within the app are processed through Google Play according to the terms presented at checkout. We do not receive payment card numbers directly. These providers may process data outside Bahrain under their own policies and configurations.

Data and image retention

We retain account data, posts and interactions while needed to provide the service or until removal through available processes. Stories disappear from the app interface after 24 hours, but this does not guarantee immediate deletion of the media or cached copies. Historical image files may remain with a previous provider until they are separately removed. Limited abuse or security records may be retained for legitimate security needs or legal obligations; hosting providers may also retain backups and logs according to their policies. We do not claim that every copy is deleted instantly. Contact support to ask about your deletion request or remaining data.

Account and data deletion

Initiate account deletion in Gamerz settings, or request it outside the app by emailing official.gamerz.app@gmail.com using the deletion instructions. We verify account ownership before processing. Deletion is intended to remove your Firebase account, profile, posts, stories, related records and images; removing shared conversations may also affect the other participant. In-app deletion depends on the deletion service completing successfully; historical media and cached copies may require separate follow-up. Limited information may be retained for applicable legal or legitimate security reasons. Submitting a request or tapping delete does not itself prove that all steps succeeded; completion must be confirmed after processing.

Your rights and security

You may request access, correction, deletion or applicable objections under relevant law. We verify identity before disclosing or modifying account data. We use authentication, access controls and HTTPS for data in transit, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not publish private information about yourself or others without permission.

Age suitability and protection of minors

Gamerz is a gaming community with user posts, reviews and direct messages; it is not exclusively for adults. Its appropriate age range is subject to the official Google Play content rating, applicable local law and safeguards for minors. Minors should involve a parent or guardian and obtain legally required consent when applicable. Abusive and exploitative content is prohibited, and users can report it or block accounts. We disclose material changes to data processing where required.

Privacy rights and contact

For privacy enquiries, data requests, and deletion: official.gamerz.app@gmail.com. See account and data deletion instructions. The email link opens your mail app; this webpage does not collect passwords, verification codes or account data.
