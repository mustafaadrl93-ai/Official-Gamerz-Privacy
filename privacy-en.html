Gamerz Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Operator and contact

Gamerz is a social app for gamers operated by an independent publisher in Bahrain. For privacy and data requests, contact official.gamerz.app@gmail.com. We may verify account ownership before acting on an account-specific request. Never send your password or verification codes.

Information we use

We use registration email and sign-in information to provide and secure accounts. Profile information includes your username, gaming platforms, chosen photos and favorites. We process posts, photos, stories, reviews, completion and self-reported achievements, comments, likes, follows, bookmarks, game libraries, tasks and ranks, as well as groups and their memberships and messages, polls, daily question answers, player status, team applications and participation and records in duels. We use this information to provide the features you choose, display results and manage the community.

We process message text, participant identifiers, timestamps and read markers, reports, feedback and moderation decisions. Device, app, network address and security identifiers may be processed to operate and protect the service. The app does not request access to contacts or precise location. We do not sell personal information or directly collect payment-card numbers. Paid memberships are not currently available for purchase.

What others can see

Members may see your public profile, public photos, posts, stories, displayed interactions and community information you choose to share. Sign-in email and passwords do not appear on public profiles. Personal libraries, bookmarks, blocked-user lists and consent records are restricted to the account owner. Post drafts are stored on your device and are not public posts. Public media may be accessible to anyone with its link, including outside Gamerz. Blocking does not retract previously shared copies or links.

Messages and privacy

In-app access to private conversations is restricted to their participants. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted; do not share secrets or highly sensitive information. Authorized moderators may access reported messages, report reasons and information needed to investigate. Group members may access group content. Poll results are displayed; poll creators cannot access other members’ individual votes through the app, including during account deletion. Votes are processed by the service to provide results and handle deletion requests. Moderation does not grant general access to browse private conversations through the app.

Service providers and international processing

We use providers for account sign-in and protection, community data storage, image and website hosting and app operations. Providers receive information necessary to perform those services and may process it outside Bahrain, including India for the current image storage service. Older images may remain with previous providers until separately removed. We may disclose necessary information to competent authorities when required or permitted by law to protect people and rights. Applicable legal requirements for data transfers and protection apply.

Data retention

We keep account information and content while necessary to provide the service or meet specific legal and security obligations. Stories leave the display after 24 hours, but images, records and backups may persist longer. Abuse reports, reasons and durations of penalties, private moderation decision records and security records may remain as necessary to investigate, prevent repeated abuse or comply with law. We cannot guarantee removal of copies independently saved outside the service.

Account and data deletion

Account owners, including suspended users, may request deletion in Gamerz, through the external deletion page or by emailing support. Requests cover the sign-in account, profile, content and associated personal data. The direct deletion process removes messages you authored, not necessarily messages written by other people; shared conversation records, notifications held by other users, older images and certain protected records may need administrative follow-up. We address remaining personal information within the scope of the request and applicable law and retain only what is necessary for a specific legitimate reason. Submitting a request is not itself confirmation of completion. Contact support to confirm completion or follow up on remaining data.

Your rights and security

You may edit available profile information and request access, correction, deletion, objection or withdrawal of consent as applicable law permits, and complain to the competent authority. We take reasonable measures to protect accounts and data in transit and storage, but no service can guarantee absolute security. Contact us with questions or privacy incident reports.

Age suitability and protection of minors

Users must meet applicable age, legal, store classification and consent requirements. Gamerz does not independently verify guardian consent; do not use the service unless you meet the applicable eligibility and consent requirements. Child sexual abuse and exploitation are prohibited. Reporting and blocking tools are available. Report safety concerns using the available in-app reporting options or contact support.

Optional crash diagnostics

You may optionally enable crash reporting in app settings. If enabled, technical crash and device information and diagnostic identifiers may be sent to Firebase Crashlytics to fix errors. We do not deliberately attach your account identifier or message content. You can disable automatic reporting in settings; disabling takes effect on the next app launch.

Changes and contact

Material changes are published here, with renewed consent when required. Privacy and account deletion requests: official.gamerz.app@gmail.com.
