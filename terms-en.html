Gamerz Terms of Use

Version: 8 November 2026 — Legal draft for review before public launch.

Acceptance and minors

Gamerz is a gaming community for reviews, stories and interactions. Tapping Accept acknowledges these Terms and the Privacy Policy. The app does not impose a blanket 18+ requirement. Minors under applicable laws must involve a parent or legal guardian and obtain any legally required guardian consent; a child's own acceptance does not replace mandatory legal permissions. Verified guardian consent and age-appropriate protections are not yet implemented in this beta, so Gamerz must not claim it is certified for children on Google Play. Declining the terms means not using community features.

Account security

Keep credentials and verification codes private. Gamerz moderators will not request passwords through posts or comments. Report suspected unauthorized access.

Prohibited content

Fraud, impersonation, requests for banking credentials, phishing, malware, threats, harassment, bullying, hate, sexual or exploitative content, privacy violations, copyright infringement and illegal activities are prohibited. Only upload content you have the right to share. Reviews, completion and platinum claims are self-reported and not independently authenticated. Game/platform marks belong to their owners and do not imply endorsement.

Moderation and blocking

Users can report posts and stories, and block other accounts. Gamerz may remove prohibited material and restrict accounts proportionately as permitted by law. The operator must take credible reports seriously and respond to known safety risks. Report threats and financial fraud to competent authorities too. Blocking cannot undo all prior sharing.

Player transactions and fraud

Gamerz is not a party to private financial transfers or trades and does not guarantee other users' identities or promises. Never send money, financial details or login codes to strangers based on an in-app profile. This does not remove the operator's liability for its own negligence or mandatory legal duties.

Paid membership

Plus and Pro checkout is not live yet. When enabled, actual prices and renewal/cancellation rules will appear through Google Play. Membership does not guarantee views, followers or identity verification.

Privacy and deletion

See the Privacy Policy. In-app deletion exists, but full automatic removal of Uploadcare media and some retained copies/interaction data is still incomplete in this beta and must be addressed before public launch.

Availability and liability

We seek reasonable security and availability but cannot guarantee continuous service or zero incidents. Users are responsible for their own misconduct and independent transactions to the extent permitted by law. Nothing excludes liability for negligence, fraud, statutory duties or non-waivable rights.

Law and contact

Applicable laws, including Bahraini law where relevant, apply. Support contact: official.gamerz.app@gmail.com. The legal publisher identity must be confirmed against the Google Play developer account before public launch. Material changes will be communicated and renewed acceptance obtained where appropriate.

Qualified Bahraini legal review is required before commercial launch.
